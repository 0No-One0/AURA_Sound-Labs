Frequently Asked Questions

Can I pair multiple AURA speakers together?

Yes. Any two AURA speakers can be paired into a true wireless stereo (L/R) setup, or up to 32 units can be linked in Party Cascade mode using the AURA Connect button.

What is your official delivery timeline?

Domestic orders are fulfilled via Bluedart and Delhivery Express (1–2 business days). Hyper-local metro delivery delivers within 4–6 hours.

How do warranty replacements work?

If hardware defects arise within 24 months, we schedule a free doorstep courier pickup and dispatch a brand-new factory-sealed replacement within 48 hours.

What is the AURA Sphere's levitation mechanism?

The AURA Sphere uses a magnetic levitation cradle that lifts the sphere ~8mm above the base, decoupling it from surface vibrations for pure acoustic isolation.
